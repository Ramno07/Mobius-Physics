Welcome to PhysicsHub

Physics Web is an educational website created for students who want to learn and understand physics in a simple, clear, and accessible way.

The website provides physics explanations, concepts, formulas, examples, and educational resources designed to help students understand difficult topics more easily. It focuses not only on memorizing equations, but also on understanding the ideas and principles behind them.
